# Brain Refine

## Purpose

Improve existing notes in the project's Second Brain without changing their original meaning or introducing unsupported information.

## Instructions

When asked to refine a note:

1. Read the complete note before making changes.
    
2. Check whether the content is clear, accurate, and properly organised.
    
3. Identify missing explanations, duplicated information, unclear statements, and outdated details.
    
4. Check links to related notes and correct broken Obsidian links where the correct destination is known.
    
5. Connect relevant project requirements, research notes, issues, and ADRs using Obsidian links.
    
6. Preserve useful technical details, examples, and source references.
    
7. Do not invent facts or silently change technical decisions.
    
8. If something is uncertain, flag it for human review instead of guessing.
    
9. Keep the writing simple, specific, and easy for team members to understand.

## Output

After reviewing a note, report:

- What was improved.
    
- What was unclear or incorrect.
    
- Which links were added or fixed.
    
- What still needs human review.
    

Do not overwrite the original note without permission.

## Quality Check

Before finishing, verify that:

- The original meaning is preserved.
    
- Technical claims are supported by sources.
    
- Obsidian links point to existing notes where possible.
    
- No unsupported assumptions were introduced.
    
- The note is readable and useful to the project team.
